# Phase 0 — Intake

## Q1 — The project, in one breath
Redesign of W Records frontend — a vinyl record store e-commerce with catalogue browsing, shopping cart, user authentication, and admin dashboard. Existing React app with routes for Catalogue, Record detail, Login/Register, Cart, and Admin.

## Q2 — Scope
**Real product.** People will rely on this regularly. Both clarity and distinctiveness matter. Accessibility and scale are in scope.

## Q3 — Risk tolerance
**5.** Middle ground — one safe direction, one ambitious, one deliberately uncomfortable.

## Q4 — Must-nots and references

**References:**
- https://www.xtcr.it/ — "mi piace ma manca personalità" (likes it but lacks personality)
- https://www.vinyl-minded.com/ — "bello ma troppo basic" (nice but too basic)

**Vibe:** "Non vorrei una cosa scontata, vorrei un layout elegante ma artistico" (don't want something predictable, want an elegant but artistic layout)

**Implicit vetoes:**
- No generic e-commerce grids without character
- No template-y record store layouts
- Must feel curated and intentional, like the records themselves
- Needs personality and artistic sensibility
